# What the Ronin Discord Is For

## Why this matters

Understand the purpose, channels, standards, and behavior expected inside the premium community.

## Clear explanation

The private community is a study room, not a signal room. Use it to improve your thinking without giving away your responsibility.

Premium members receive access to the private Ronin Charts Discord. The community is an extension of the course: a place to study charts, ask questions, review decisions, and build consistency with traders using the same language.

It is **not** a signal room.

### What belongs in the community

- Market-condition observations supported by charts
- Flat Base Breakout studies
- Controlled Pullback studies
- Questions about key levels, volume, relative strength, and triggers
- Pre-trade plans that explain the setup and invalidation
- Post-trade reviews that separate process from outcome
- Examples of rejected trades and lessons from waiting
- Course questions and technical support

### What does not belong

- “Buy this now” messages
- Guaranteed return claims
- Pumping illiquid stocks
- Copying another member’s position without independent analysis
- Posting private account, payment, or personal information
- Insults, harassment, or status competition
- Sharing premium course content outside the community

### Suggested channel structure

Your server administrator can rename channels, but a practical structure is:

- `#welcome`
- `#announcements`
- `#course-questions`
- `#market-context`
- `#flat-base-study`
- `#pullback-study`
- `#watchlist-workflow`
- `#trade-review`
- `#platform-support`

### The Ronin posting standard

A useful chart post answers:

1. What is the market condition?
2. Why is the stock a leader?
3. Which parent setup is present?
4. What is the key level?
5. What confirmation is still required?
6. What would make the chart invalid?

This format creates discussion based on process rather than excitement.

## Real example or short trading story

A member posts a chart with the market regime, parent setup, key level, required confirmation, and invalidation. The discussion focuses on whether the rule is complete.

Another member posts only, “What should I buy today?” That turns the group into a signal room.

The Ronin Discord is built for the first kind of conversation: study, preparation, review, and independent judgment.

## Key rules / steps

1. **Use the Discord for study, preparation, review, and technical support.**
2. **Post with the Ronin standard.** Include regime, leadership, parent setup, key level, confirmation, and invalidation.
3. **Keep signals, guarantees, pumping, and copied trades out of the community.**
4. **Protect private member content.**
5. **Ask for feedback on reasoning, not permission to trade.**

**Rule to remember**

> Ask for feedback on your reasoning, not permission to take a trade.

The long-term objective is independence. The community should make your process stronger, not make you dependent on another trader’s opinion.

## Common mistakes

- Turning the group into a signal room
- Posting private member content outside the community
- Celebrating reckless winners while ignoring broken rules

### Coach's note

Watch for **social proof and herd behavior**. Community can improve learning or weaken independence. Write your own plan before reading other opinions.
