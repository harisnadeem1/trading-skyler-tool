# Welcome to the Ronin Trading System

## Why this matters

This course teaches one repeatable way to trade strong US stocks. You first find strength, then wait for either a flat base or a controlled pullback, and only act after the chart confirms that buyers are returning.

## Clear explanation

You are building a decision process, not collecting isolated tricks. Learn the order first. The details only become useful when they fit that order.

The Ronin Trading System is a **long-only swing trading framework for US-listed stocks**. It is designed for one job: finding market leaders, waiting for those leaders to form understandable structures, and acting only when price confirms that demand is returning.

The system is deliberately narrow. It does not attempt to trade every market condition or every chart pattern. Every position must belong to one of two parent setups:

1. **Flat Base Breakout**
2. **Controlled Pullback**

Everything else is context, confirmation, or trade management.

### The central sequence

The repeated market behavior behind both setups is:

> **Strength → Expansion → Contraction → Support → Confirmation → Continuation**

A stock must first prove that it can attract meaningful demand. That proof may be an earnings gap, a high-volume event, a breakout from a long accumulation area, or sustained relative-strength leadership. After the proof, I wait for the stock to rest.

The rest is not wasted time. It is where supply is tested. A leader that can pause without giving back much of its advance is telling me that holders are not eager to sell.

When volume contracts and price tightens near support, the stock may be preparing for another expansion.

### What this system is not

This is not a prediction service, a collection of alerts, or a promise that every setup will work. It is not built around buying any stock that touches a moving average. It is not a shortcut around chart study.

It is a framework for making the same quality of decision repeatedly:

- Is the market supportive?
- Is this stock a genuine leader?
- Has it already demonstrated institutional demand?
- Is the current structure one of my two setups?
- Is supply contracting?
- Is there a clear key level?
- Has demand returned?
- Can I define where the idea is wrong?

### The Ronin mindset

A disciplined trader does not need constant action. He needs clarity.

When the conditions are incomplete, the correct decision is to wait. When the setup is clean but the market is hostile, the correct decision may still be to wait. When the trigger is gone and price is extended, the correct decision is to let it go.

The system becomes powerful when you stop judging yourself by how many trades you take and start judging yourself by how faithfully you follow the process.

### Your responsibility as a student

Study every lesson, but do not accept any rule blindly. Save chart examples. Compare winners with failures.

Review how the same setup behaves in different market regimes. Test the framework using historical charts and a simulator before committing meaningful capital.

The goal is not to memorize definitions. The goal is to develop pattern recognition so strong that poor structures become obvious and high-quality structures feel familiar.

### Ronin checklist

Before moving forward, commit to these principles:

- I will trade only the setups defined by the system
- I will separate a strong stock from a valid entry
- I will use the weekly chart for context, the daily chart for the setup, and the 30-minute chart for execution
- I will not confuse a moving-average touch with confirmation
- I will treat cash and patience as valid positions
- I will review my decisions without excuses

**Strength first. Structure second. Confirmation before execution.**

### How to apply it in real time

The Ronin Trading System is a decision framework, not a collection of alerts. Its purpose is to narrow the market until only a small number of situations deserve attention. The framework is long-only, focused on liquid US-listed stocks, and built around two parent setups: the Flat Base Breakout and the Controlled Pullback.

Every indicator, timeframe, and rule exists to improve the selection or execution of those two patterns.

The system begins with acceptance of uncertainty. A valid setup can fail, and an invalid trade can temporarily make money. Therefore, you are judged first by process: whether the market condition was appropriate, whether the stock was a genuine leader, whether the structure qualified, and whether the trigger appeared where planned.

The outcome matters over a series of trades, but it cannot be allowed to rewrite the rules after one result.

The system also has boundaries. It does not attempt to catch bottoms, trade every earnings reaction, predict index turns, or turn moving averages into automatic signals. It waits for evidence of demand, then for supply to contract, and finally for demand to return.

## Real example or short trading story

A new student opens ten charts and sees ten different ideas: a bounce, a gap, a moving-average touch, a breakout, and a stock that simply looks cheap.

The Ronin system removes that confusion. The student asks one question first: **Is this a Flat Base Breakout or a Controlled Pullback in a proven leader?** If the answer is no, the chart is not a trade for this system.

That narrow focus feels restrictive at first. In practice, it is what makes repetition and real pattern recognition possible.

## Key rules / steps

1. Classify the broad market before opening individual charts.
2. Restrict attention to liquid leaders with real momentum or a meaningful catalyst.
3. Label the daily pattern as Flat Base, Controlled Pullback, or No Setup.
4. Use the weekly chart for context and the 30-minute chart only for tactical confirmation.
5. Record the decision so the process can be reviewed independently of profit or loss.

**Rule to remember**

> **The system has only two parent setups. When a chart is neither a Flat Base Breakout nor a Controlled Pullback, it is a no-trade chart.**

## Common mistakes

- Trying to memorize everything at once instead of learning the decision order
- Treating every strong green candle as a Ronin setup
- Adding extra setups whenever patience becomes uncomfortable
- Using the language of the system while ignoring its sequence
- Judging the framework after a handful of trades

### Coach's note

Watch for **uncertainty and responsibility**. The mind looks for certainty, but trading offers probabilities. Your job is to own the quality of the decision without demanding a particular outcome.
