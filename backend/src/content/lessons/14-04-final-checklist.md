# Final Ronin Checklist and Certification

## Why this matters

Certification means you can explain the framework, classify charts consistently, and reject invalid trades without needing someone else to decide for you.

## Clear explanation

This module connects the entire system into one operating process. You should be able to explain every decision before you take it.

Before considering the course complete, you should be able to answer every question below without guessing.

### Market

- What regime is active?
- What evidence supports the label?
- Are leaders and breakouts working?
- What would upgrade or downgrade the view?

### Stock

- Why is this stock a leader?
- What is the catalyst?
- Is the group strong?
- Is RS leading?
- Is the weekly chart constructive?

### Setup

- Is this Flat Base or Controlled Pullback?
- What is the prior expansion?
- Is volume contracting?
- What is the key level?
- What invalidates the pattern?

### Entry

- What confirmation is required?
- Is the 30-minute trigger clear?
- What is LOD ATR %?
- What is entry-to-stop ATR?
- Is the stock extended?

### Risk

- Is planned risk within 0.5%?
- Is position value within 15%?
- Is the stop structural?
- Is portfolio heat acceptable?
- Are positions correlated?

### Management

- What behavior means the trade is failing?
- What structure will trail the position?
- Under what conditions will you trim?
- How will adds affect maximum committed risk?

### Review

- Did you preserve the original plan?
- Did you follow the rules?
- Was the result driven by process or luck?
- What evidence should update the playbook?

### Certification exercise

Complete the following before using meaningful size:

1. Build twenty qualified Flat Base examples.
2. Build twenty qualified Controlled Pullback examples.
3. Build twenty rejected examples for each setup.
4. Replay at least fifty historical triggers bar by bar.
5. Journal at least thirty simulated or minimum-size trades.
6. Demonstrate rule compliance over a meaningful sample.

Completion does not guarantee profitability. It proves that you understand the framework well enough to test it responsibly.

### How to apply it in real time

Completion of the lessons is not proof of mastery. Certification in the Ronin framework means you can classify market conditions, identify genuine leaders, distinguish the two parent setups from look-alikes, map levels, evaluate volume, recognize a valid trigger, calculate risk, and review the decision without hindsight.

Before using meaningful size, the student should build a documented sample in replay, simulation, or very small risk. The sample should include accepted trades, rejected trades, and correctly managed losses. A high win rate is not the only goal; consistent application and positive expectancy over a meaningful series matter more.

The final standard is independence. You should be able to explain why a setup qualifies without relying on another person’s alert. They should also be able to say “no trade” confidently when one critical requirement is missing.

## Real example or short trading story

A setup looks excellent, but the final checklist reveals an earnings date you missed and a Low-of-Day distance beyond the preferred threshold.

The checklist is not paperwork. It is the last defense against selective attention.

You use it to confirm market, stock, setup, entry, risk, and review before capital is committed.

## Key rules / steps

1. Complete a written exam on definitions, formulas, and failure conditions.
2. Classify at least one hundred hidden-outcome charts.
3. Build twenty complete trade plans before the trigger occurs.
4. Execute a defined sample in simulation or minimal size with full journaling.
5. Demonstrate compliance with market, setup, entry, risk, and review rules.
6. Produce a personal playbook containing only evidence-supported variations.
7. Define the conditions required before increasing size.

**Rule to remember**

> **Do not trade meaningful size until you can follow the complete checklist consistently.**

## Common mistakes

- Using a checklist mechanically without understanding why each item exists
- Treating course completion as permission to trade large
- Using profit in a small sample as the only certification measure
- Avoiding failed examples because they reduce confidence
- Depending on external opinions to validate every setup

### Coach's note

Watch for **consistency under pressure**. Mastery means the same decision sequence is used when excited, afraid, bored, winning, or losing.
