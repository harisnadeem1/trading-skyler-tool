# Advanced Milestone: Final Ronin Mastery Assessment

## Why this matters

The final checkpoint tests whether the system operates as one process. Technical knowledge is not enough. The student must select correctly, execute consistently, manage risk, control behavior, and review without rewriting history.

## Clear explanation

This checkpoint tests application, not memory. Work without future candles, outside opinions, or hindsight.

This module connects the entire system into one operating process. You should be able to explain every decision before you take it.

### Required certification project

Complete a **30-chart mastery portfolio**:

- 10 valid Flat Base Breakouts
- 10 valid Controlled Pullbacks
- 10 rejected or failed structures

Each chart must include:

- market regime
- leadership and catalyst evidence
- weekly context
- daily setup classification
- key levels and moving averages
- volume and RS analysis
- 30-minute trigger or reason no trigger existed
- LOD ATR and stop-distance calculation when relevant
- structural invalidation
- psychological risk present in the decision
- final process grade

### Simulated execution requirement

Complete at least twenty replay or simulated trades using the same written checklist. Track:

- rule compliance
- average planned risk
- entry efficiency
- hesitation or chase events
- management decisions
- result in R
- process grade

The objective is not a specific profit. The objective is stable execution.

### Personal operating plan

Write a one-page plan containing:

- market-regime rules
- stock-selection filters
- Flat Base checklist
- Controlled Pullback checklist
- entry and no-chase rules
- stop and position-size rules
- maximum portfolio heat
- drawdown protocol
- management framework
- daily and weekly review routine
- emotional reset protocol

### Completion standard

Certification requires:

- completion of every core lesson in Levels 1–3
- all module quizzes passed at **80% or higher**
- Beginner and Intermediate milestones passed
- final milestone score of **85% or higher**
- completion of the chart portfolio and personal operating plan

The Academy can track lesson and quiz completion. The student remains responsible for completing the chart work honestly.

## Real example or short trading story

For the final project, you build a complete personal operating plan and execute a sample of trades in simulation or replay.

You must show the market regime, leadership evidence, parent setup, trigger, risk calculation, management, psychology notes, and review for each decision.

Certification is not based on one profitable result. It is based on whether you can apply the full system consistently and explain your reasoning.

## Key rules / steps

1. **Complete the 30-chart portfolio.** Include 10 valid Flat Base Breakouts, 10 valid Controlled Pullbacks, and 10 rejected or failed structures.
2. **Complete at least 20 replay or simulated trades.** Use the same written checklist and record process grades.
3. **Write the one-page personal operating plan.** Include market, selection, setup, entry, risk, management, review, and reset rules.
4. **Meet the completion standards.** Pass module quizzes at 80% or higher, earlier milestones, and the final assessment at 85% or higher.
5. **Prove repeatable process quality.** Profit alone does not qualify as mastery.

**Rule to remember**

> Mastery is the ability to repeat the complete process when the market, the outcome, and your emotions are uncertain.

## Common mistakes

- Treating one profitable sample as proof of mastery
- Submitting incomplete records
- Avoiding losing or rejected examples in the final review
