# Install the Four Required Indicators

## Why this matters

Install the official scripts and understand why the system uses each one.

## Clear explanation

Your TradingView workspace should make the Ronin process easier to see. Indicators support the decision; they do not make the decision.

These four TradingView scripts do different jobs. None of them is a buy signal. Together they help you answer four practical questions:

1. **Is the stock liquid and capable of moving?**
2. **Is volume confirming or drying up?**
3. **Where are the key daily and weekly trend references?**
4. **Is price extended or is the entry already too far from the Low of Day?**

### How to install any TradingView community indicator

1. Log in to TradingView.
2. Open the indicator link below.
3. Select **Use on chart** or add it to your favorites.
4. Return to your chart and open **Indicators**.
5. Search the exact script name if it did not open automatically.
6. Add it once, open **Settings**, and apply the Ronin configuration.
7. Save the complete indicator template as **Ronin Full**.

Do not copy or republish another creator’s Pine source. Use the original TradingView pages so updates remain connected to the creator.

### 1. ATR% multiple from 50-MA

**Install:** [ATR% multiple from 50-MA](https://www.tradingview.com/script/oimVgV7e-ATR-multiple-from-50-MA/)

### What it measures

The script compares the stock’s percentage gain from the 50-day moving average with its ATR percentage. Put simply, it asks:

> How far has price stretched away from the 50-MA compared with the stock’s normal volatility?

### Why the Ronin system uses it

A stock can remain strong while becoming dangerously extended. The indicator helps separate normal strength from a move that may be entering a late, vertical phase.

### How we use it

- It is an **extension and trade-management reference**, not an entry trigger
- Values around **7–10 ATR% multiples above the 50-MA** deserve closer review for extension, stalling, or partial profit-taking
- A high value does not require an automatic sale. Read price, volume, market conditions, and the stock’s structure
- A low value does not make a weak chart attractive

### 2. MarketSmith Volumes

**Install:** [MarketSmith Volumes](https://www.tradingview.com/script/DRT8QZSp/)

### What it shows

The script presents volume in a MarketSmith-like format and includes:

- a 50-day average-volume line on daily charts
- a 10-week average-volume line on weekly charts
- high-volume peaks
- optional current volume and volume-buzz labels
- average volume, average dollar volume, and Up/Down Volume ratio
- HVE and HV1 labels
- optional low-relative-volume highlighting

### Why the Ronin system uses it

Both Ronin setups depend on a change in supply and demand. We want:

- strong volume during the original expansion
- quieter volume during the base or pullback
- renewed participation when price resumes higher
- immediate visibility of HVE or HV1 days that may signal institutional urgency

### How we use it

- **Flat base:** look for the general volume trend to contract, especially in the final part of the base
- **Controlled pullback:** prefer declining volume while price retreats toward support
- **Breakout or reversal:** look for participation to improve as price confirms
- **Liquidity:** use average dollar volume to reject names that may be difficult to enter or exit
- **HVE/HV1:** add the stock to research; do not treat the label as an automatic entry

### 3. Moving Averages For All Timeframes [LevelUp]

**Install:** [Moving Averages For All Timeframes](https://www.tradingview.com/script/fxAXf8hu-Moving-Averages-For-All-Timeframes-LevelUp/)

### What it does

This script manages moving averages from several timeframes inside one indicator. It can display daily and weekly averages together, choose different moving-average types, and show a weekly average on a daily chart.

### Ronin configuration

Use:

- **Daily 8 EMA** — immediate momentum and primary pullback reference
- **Daily 21 EMA** — intermediate trend support
- **Daily 50 EMA** — broader structural trend reference
- **Daily 200 SMA** — long-term trend reference
- **Weekly 8 EMA on the daily chart** — higher-timeframe support, colored separately so it cannot be confused with the daily 8 EMA

### How we use it

Moving averages define areas of interest, not mechanical buy points. A pullback becomes actionable only when the level aligns with price structure, volume contraction, and renewed strength.

### 4. Swing Data — ADR% / RVol / PVol / Float / Avg $ Vol

**Install:** [Swing Data](https://www.tradingview.com/script/uloAa2EI-Swing-Data-ADR-RVol-PVol-Float-Avg-Vol/)

### What it shows

Depending on the settings and symbol, the table can display:

- market capitalization
- float and float percentage
- ADR% and ATR%
- Low-of-Day distance
- average daily volume and average dollar volume
- projected volume and relative volume
- liquidity cap
- RS Rating
- Up/Down Volume ratio
- distance from 52-week highs and lows
- sector and industry information

### Why the Ronin system uses it

It compresses the practical information needed for selection and execution into one small table.

### How we use it

- **ADR%:** confirms that the stock moves enough to justify a swing trade
- **Average dollar volume:** confirms liquidity
- **RS Rating and sector data:** confirms leadership and context
- **RVol / PVol:** shows whether live participation is above or below normal
- **Low-of-Day distance:** measures entry efficiency. The Ronin preference is generally to enter while the distance remains below approximately **60% of daily ATR**
- **ATR%:** helps compare volatility across stocks

### Avoid indicator overload

Do not add all table fields at full size. Keep only values that influence the decision. The chart must remain readable on a laptop screen.

## Real example or short trading story

You install an indicator and immediately see a label that looks bullish. The temptation is to treat it as a signal.

Instead, you ask what job the tool performs. MarketSmith Volumes helps you read participation. Swing Data organizes ADR%, liquidity, RS, RVol/PVol, and Low-of-Day distance.

The multi-timeframe MA tool shows the daily and weekly averages. The ATR multiple tool provides extension context.

None of them replaces market condition, setup structure, confirmation, or risk.

## Key rules / steps

1. **Install each script from the exact TradingView link in the lesson.**
2. **Configure the tool for its defined Ronin job.** Do not display unused fields.
3. **Verify the information on both parent setups.** Confirm that the tool supports, rather than replaces, the chart decision.
4. **Keep the layout readable on a laptop screen.**
5. **Save the completed template as Ronin Full.**

**Rule to remember**

> A clean workspace improves attention, but no indicator can replace market context, price structure, or independent judgment.

## Common mistakes

- Treating an indicator label as an entry signal
- Installing unofficial copies without checking the source
- Displaying every possible field until the chart becomes unreadable
