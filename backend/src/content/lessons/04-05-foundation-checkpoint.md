# Foundation Milestone: Market, Leaders, and Mindset

## Why this matters

This checkpoint closes the Beginner level. It tests whether you can identify the correct market, choose the correct stocks, interpret relative strength and catalysts, and maintain the patience required to wait for structure.

## Clear explanation

This checkpoint tests application, not memory. Work without future candles, outside opinions, or hindsight.

An earnings gap, HVE, or HV1 event can reveal institutional urgency. It earns the stock your attention. It does not create an automatic entry.

### Required evidence before completion

Complete the following before taking the milestone quiz:

1. Classify ten recent market sessions using the four Ronin regimes.
2. Build one leadership list and one actionable-soon list.
3. Annotate five stocks showing constructive RS and five showing deteriorating RS.
4. Review five earnings or HVE events and identify the first later structure.
5. Record three correct no-trade decisions and the emotion that was controlled.

### Foundation case study

Select one stock from the leadership list and write:

- current market regime
- reason the stock is a leader
- catalyst or evidence of institutional demand
- current RS condition
- whether the daily chart is a Flat Base, Controlled Pullback, Developing, or No Setup
- the next condition required before it becomes actionable
- the strongest disqualifying evidence

Do not write an entry plan yet. This checkpoint tests selection and context, not execution.

## Real example or short trading story

You receive a chart with the ticker and future candles hidden. Your task is to classify the market, judge leadership, identify the catalyst, and decide whether the stock belongs on the watchlist.

There is no reward for guessing the future outcome. The checkpoint measures whether your reasoning is complete before the outcome is visible.

If you cannot explain the evidence, you are not ready to move to setup execution yet.

## Key rules / steps

1. **Classify the market regime from current evidence.**
2. **Identify genuine leadership, catalyst quality, and relative strength.**
3. **Build or reject the watchlist candidate without seeing future candles.**
4. **Explain the psychological pressure that could weaken the decision.**
5. **Pass the milestone assessment before moving to setup execution.**

**Rule to remember**

> The right setup begins with the right environment, the right stock, and the discipline to wait.

## Common mistakes

- Using future candles to justify the classification
- Passing a stock because the story sounds exciting
- Moving forward without being able to explain market regime and leadership
