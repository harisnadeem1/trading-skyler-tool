    Required tools

    Four indicators. Four separate jobs.

    Install each script from its original TradingView page. The scripts are third-party tools and may be changed, protected, or removed by their authors.
    01

    ATR% Multiple from 50-MA

    Why: measures extension from the 50-MA relative to normal volatility.

    Ronin use: extension and trade-management context. It is not an entry signal and not a top predictor.

    Install on TradingView ↗
    02

    MarketSmith Volumes

    Why: makes expansion, dry-up, HVE/HV1 events, liquidity, and volume averages easy to read.

    Ronin use: strong volume into expansion, quieter volume during contraction, and renewed participation at confirmation.

    Install on TradingView ↗
    03

    Moving Averages for All Timeframes

    Why: displays daily and weekly trend references inside one controlled indicator.

    Ronin use: daily 8 EMA, 21 EMA, 50 EMA, 200 SMA, and the weekly 8 EMA on the daily chart.

    Install on TradingView ↗
    04

    Swing Data

    Why: puts ADR%, RVol/PVol, float, liquidity, RS, ATR%, and Low-of-Day distance in one compact table.

    Ronin use: stock selection, liquidity checks, leadership context, and entry-efficiency review.

    Install on TradingView ↗

    Use the tools in order

    Weekly context → Daily setup → 30-minute trigger.

    1. Confirm the larger trend and overhead supply.
    2. Classify the daily chart as Flat Base Breakout, Controlled Pullback, or No Setup.
    3. Read volume, RS, ADR%, liquidity, and key levels.
    4. Use the 30-minute chart only to refine a valid daily setup.
    5. Reject an entry that has become late, extended, or structurally unclear.

    Community standard

    Independence before imitation.

    The group exists to strengthen observation, preparation, and review. Every member remains fully responsible for every trading decision.

    • No “buy this now” or guaranteed-return posts.
    • No pumping illiquid stocks or copying another member’s size.
    • No sharing premium course material outside the group.
    • Refer to a chart rule when offering feedback.
    • Respect privacy, uncertainty, and different experience levels.
